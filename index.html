Impossible Twist
Drag to twist

How to twist

The structure always comes to rest in one of 24 positions. From some of them, separate floors line up and look joined.

Mouse or touch

Drag left / right
Turn the structure around.
Drag up / down
Tip it over. The side under your pointer rolls up to become the floor, or down to become a wall.
Let go
It settles into the nearest position. A quick flick carries it to the next one.

Structures
